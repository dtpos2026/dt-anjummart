7-day return and exchange

If something is wrong with your order, contact us within 7 days of delivery. If the parcel was damaged in transit, please tell us within 48 hours and send photos or an unboxing video.

What we will replace or refund

  • The watch arrived damaged or defective.
  • You received a different model, colour or variant from the one you ordered.
  • Parts or accessories listed with your order are missing.

For these cases we send a replacement at our cost, including the return courier charges. If a replacement is not possible, we refund you.

Change of mind

Within 7 days of delivery you may ask to exchange an unused, unworn watch for another item of ours. It must be in its original box with all papers and accessories. For change-of-mind exchanges the customer pays the courier charges both ways, and we do not give cash refunds.

What cannot be returned

  • Watches that show signs of wear, scratches, dents, water damage or misuse.
  • Watches that have been opened, adjusted or repaired by anyone other than us (including strap or bracelet link removal).
  • Items returned without the original box, papers or accessories.

How to start a return

  1. WhatsApp or call +92 371 4661514, or email anjummart1985@gmail.com with your order number and clear photos or a video.
  2. We review your request and reply with the next steps. Please do not send the parcel before we confirm.
  3. After we receive and inspect the item we send the replacement or issue the refund.

Refunds

Approved refunds are paid within 7–10 working days after we receive and inspect the item. For Cash on Delivery orders we refund by bank transfer or mobile wallet in the name of the person who placed the order. Orders paid online are refunded to the original payment method.

Cancelling an order

You can cancel an order free of charge before it is dispatched. Message us on WhatsApp as soon as possible with your order number.

Warranty

Any warranty offered on a product is stated on its product page. Unless stated, the 7-day policy above applies.
