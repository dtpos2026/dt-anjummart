Delivery area

Anjum Mart delivers all over Pakistan through courier partners. We do not ship outside Pakistan at this time.

Delivery charges: FREE

Delivery is free on every order, to every city in Pakistan. The checkout shows “Free Delivery (All Pakistan)” and there is nothing extra to pay for shipping.

Order confirmation

After you place an order we may confirm it by phone call or WhatsApp on the number you gave at checkout. Please keep your phone reachable and make sure your name, full address, city and phone number are correct. Orders that cannot be confirmed or delivered because of a wrong or unreachable address or phone number may be put on hold or cancelled.

Dispatch and delivery time

  • Orders are dispatched within 1–2 working days after confirmation (Monday to Saturday, excluding public holidays).
  • Delivery usually takes 3–5 working days to major cities and 5–7 working days to other areas.
  • These are estimates, not guarantees. Courier delays, weather, strikes and public holidays can add time.

Payment on delivery

Cash on Delivery (COD) is available. You pay the courier in cash when you receive the parcel. Other payment methods, if any, are shown at checkout.

Advance Cash Payment (Rs. 499 off)

As an alternative to Cash on Delivery you can choose Advance Cash Payment in your cart and pay by Meezan Bank transfer. The Rs. 499 advance payment discount is then applied to your order. Enter the transaction ID in your cart and send a screenshot of the payment to us on WhatsApp +92 371 4661514.

Your order is confirmed only after we verify your payment. Until then the order is not marked as paid and is not dispatched. A transaction ID or screenshot on its own does not confirm a payment.

Tracking

When the courier provides a tracking number we send it to you by WhatsApp, SMS or email.

When your parcel arrives

Please check that the parcel is sealed and undamaged before accepting it. If it looks opened or damaged, tell the courier and contact us straight away. We recommend recording a short unboxing video when you open the parcel; it helps us resolve any claim quickly.
