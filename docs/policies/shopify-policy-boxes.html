1Return and refund policy

No returns accepted

All sales are final. We do not accept returns, exchanges or refunds for change of mind, wrong size or colour preference, or because the item looks different on your screen. Please read the product details and look at the photos carefully before you order. This applies to all watches, jewellery sets and perfumes.

Damaged or wrong item: report within 48 hours

If your parcel arrives damaged, or you receive a different item from the one you ordered, tell us within 48 hours of delivery and send clear photos or an unboxing video. After we check the claim we send a replacement of the same item at our cost. If the same item is no longer in stock we refund you.

We recommend recording a short video when you open the parcel. It is the quickest way to settle a claim.

What is not covered

  • Damage, scratches, water damage or wear after delivery.
  • Items that have been opened, adjusted or repaired by anyone other than us (including bracelet link removal).
  • Claims reported after 48 hours without photos or video.

How to report a problem

  1. WhatsApp or call +92 371 4661514, or email anjummart1985@gmail.com with your order number and photos or a video.
  2. We review your claim and reply with the next steps. Please do not send the parcel before we confirm.
  3. We send the replacement, or refund where a replacement is not possible.

Refunds

Where a refund is approved it is paid within 7–10 working days. For Cash on Delivery orders we refund by bank transfer or mobile wallet in the name of the person who placed the order. Orders paid online are refunded to the original payment method.

Advance payments

If an order paid in advance (Advance Cash Payment) is cancelled before it is dispatched, or we cannot supply the item, we refund the amount you paid by bank transfer or mobile wallet within 7–10 working days, like any other refund. If a payment cannot be matched to your order, message us on WhatsApp with the transaction ID and your order number.

Cancelling an order

You can cancel an order free of charge before it is dispatched. Message us on WhatsApp as soon as possible with your order number.

Delivery is free on every order, to every city in Pakistan. The checkout shows “Free Delivery (All Pakistan)” and there is nothing extra to pay for shipping.

After you place an order we may confirm it by phone call or WhatsApp on the number you gave at checkout.
