4. Launch se pehle (is hafte)

  • Naya theme "Anjum Mart Champagne Final" publish karein, phir ek phone par poora order test karein (cart se checkout tak, COD).
  • Storefront password hata dein (agar laga hai). Order #1005 ka confirmation email check karein.
  • Policies Shopify Settings mein paste karein (Shipping, Return/Refund, Terms) aur Privacy policy ki contact line theek karein.
  • Facebook & Instagram sales channel install karein (Meta Pixel aur Conversions API ke liye) aur Events Manager mein ek test Purchase dekhein. Ye ads se pehle zaroori hai.
  • Google & YouTube channel install karein, Search Console verify karein, sitemap submit karein: anjummart.com/sitemap.xml
  • Teeno profiles naye logo, bio aur website link ke saath tayyar. WhatsApp Business app mein catalog aur quick replies (price, delivery, COD, replacement policy).
  • Courier chunein aur COD rates, pickup aur parcel ki tracking ka tareeqa pakka karein. Free delivery ka matlab courier ka kharcha aap ki margin mein hai.
  • Packaging: saaf box, gift ke liye achi presentation, ek chhota "thank you" card jis par WhatsApp number ho.
  • Website par Instagram, Facebook aur TikTok ke links lagwayein (Theme settings, Social media). Links bhej dein, main laga dunga.

5. Channel plan

WhatsApp (sab se zyada orders yahin se aayenge)

  • Har product ka WhatsApp Business catalog; har inquiry ka jawab jaldi (pehle ghante mein) aur ek hi lehje mein.
  • Broadcast sirf unhein jinhon ne khud ijazat di ho. Roz status par ek product ya ek customer ki tasveer (ijazat se).
  • Har COD order ki confirmation: order aate hi call ya WhatsApp par address aur number confirm karein, phir dispatch. Isse jhoote aur galat orders aur wapas aane wale parcels kam hote hain.

Instagram aur Facebook

  • Hafte mein 4 se 5 posts: 2 Reels, 1 carousel (product aur price), 1 customer ya unboxing post, roz Stories.
  • Highlights: Men, Ladies, Jewellery, Delivery aur COD, Replacement policy, Customer photos.
  • Bio mein link anjummart.com; har post ke caption mein price, "free delivery all over Pakistan" aur order ka tareeqa.

TikTok

  • Hafte mein 5 se 7 chhoti videos (15 se 30 second): wrist shot, dial close-up, packing, unboxing, "Rs 3,999 mein ye watch", jewellery set outfit ke saath.
  • Business account par sirf commercial-use wali music istemal karein (TikTok ki Commercial Music Library).

Google

  • Search Console connect karein; products Google par aane mein waqt lagta hai. Free listings ke liye Merchant Center baad mein, jab pixel aur policies theek hon.

Creators (micro influencers)

  • Chhote creators (apni audience wale, 5,000 se 50,000 followers) ko product de kar video karwayein. Har sponsored video mein #ad ya "paid partnership" label lagana zaroori hai. Stock sirf 10 pieces har model ka hai, is liye ek waqt mein 2 se 3 creators se zyada na karein.

8. Offers: sach par mabni

  • Jo pehle se sach hai wahi bechein: free delivery poore Pakistan, Cash on Delivery, 48 ghante mein damaged/galat item ka replacement. Ye teeno har ad aur post mein likhein.
  • Bundle (aap ki marzi, margin dekh kar): jaise "couple set" (men's + ladies' watch) ya "watch + gift packaging". Qeemat bilkul asli ho; jhoote "pehle ki qeemat" ya "aaj hi last din" jaise dawe na karein jab tak wo sach na hon.
  • Stock ki baat: "sirf X pieces bachay" tab likhein jab system mein sach mein itne hon. Nakli urgency na banayein.

9. Customer ka safar aur bharosa

  1. Order aaya (website ya WhatsApp) → 1 se 2 ghante mein call ya WhatsApp par confirmation.
  2. Dispatch → tracking number customer ko bhejein.
  3. Delivery ke baad (2 se 3 din) → "kaisa laga?" ka message; khush customer se tasveer aur chhota review maangein, aur use post karne ki ijazat lein.
  4. Dobara khareedna → 30 din baad naye arrivals ka message; jewellery khareedne walon ko watch aur watch walon ko gift ka idea.
Kabhi na karein: nakli reviews, nakli "abhi kisi ne khareeda" ya "X logon ne dekha" popups, doosron ki tasveerein ya videos apni batana, "100% original ya authentic" ka daawa jab tak saboot na ho, "return" ka wada jab policy mein return nahi hai. Ye customer ko dhoka hai, aur isse Meta ya Google ke ads reject ya account restrict ho sakte hain.

Store mein "Verified order" popup ka system maujood hai, magar wo sirf tab dikhata hai jab aap asli order (shehar, product, waqt) list mein daalein. Detail: docs/SOCIAL-PROOF.md.
