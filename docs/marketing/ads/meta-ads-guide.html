2. Aap ka sawal: 1 campaign, 1 ad set, 4–5 creatives?

Jawab: 1 campaign rakhein, lekin 2 ad sets. Wajah: Women ki ads sirf Instagram par jayengi aur Men ki Instagram + Facebook par. Placements ad set ki setting hoti hai, isliye dono ko alag ad set chahiye. Har ad set mein 4–5 creatives (ads) rakhein.

3. Ads Manager mein step by step

Meta ke buttons ke naam kabhi kabhi thore badal jate hain, lekin tarteeb yehi hai.

  1. business.facebook.com/adsmanager kholen → Create.
  2. Objective: Engagement (ya jahan "Messages" ka option aaye). Conversion location: Messaging apps → WhatsApp. Kuch accounts mein "Sales" objective ke andar bhi "Messaging apps" milta hai, dono se WhatsApp ad hi banta hai.
  3. WhatsApp number connect karein (wohi number jo customers ko dete hain, +92 371 4661514). Meta verification code bhejega.
  4. Ad set 1 (Women): Location Pakistan; Gender Women; Age (misal: 21–45, apne customers ke hisaab se badal lein). Placements: Manual placements → sirf Instagram (Feed, Stories, Reels). Daily budget aur 7 din ki schedule.
  5. Ad set 2 (Men): Location Pakistan; Gender Men; Age (misal: 21–50). Placements: Instagram + Facebook. Budget women wale ke barabar.
  6. Har ad mein: Facebook Page aur Instagram account chunein; image upload karein; Primary text, Headline, Description us creative ke CAPTIONS file se paste karein; Call to action: WhatsApp message.
  7. Message template: Greeting mein ye likhein: "Assalamualaikum! Anjum Mart mein khush aamdeed. Kaunsi watch pasand aayi? Apna naam aur city bata dein, hum order confirm kar dete hain." Ice breakers (customer ek tap se bhej sakta hai): Price kya hai? · Delivery kitne din mein hogi? · Cash on Delivery hai? · Order kaise karun?
  8. Review → Publish. Meta ka review aam taur par kuch ghante leta hai. Reject ho to "Request review" dabayein.

4. Boost aur Ads Manager: dono kaise

  • Boost (Instagram/Facebook app se "Boost post"): aasan hai, lekin control kam. Goal "More messages" chunein.
  • Ads Manager: poora control (placements, budget, 2 ad sets). Asli test iske zariye chalayein.
  • Dono ek hi post par na chalayein. Warna aap ke apne ads aapas mein muqabla karenge aur paisa double kharch hoga. Behtar: asli test Ads Manager se; organic posts normal post karein; boost sirf ek-do aise posts par chhote budget se karein jo Ads Manager mein nahi chal rahe.
  • Organic post karte waqt CAPTIONS file ka caption aur hashtags copy karein (hashtags 8–12 rakhein, caption ke neeche).

5. Pehle hafte ka rozana kaam

  • Har WhatsApp message ka kuch minute mein jawab dein. WhatsApp-Quick-Replies.txt use karein.
  • Rozana sirf dekhein: cost per message aur kaun si creative zyada messages la rahi hai. 3–4 din tak ads na badlein.
  • Ek sheet mein likhein: kitne messages → kitne asli orders. Ads ka asli nateeja cost per order hai, sirf messages nahi.
  • Hafte ke baad: jin creatives se orders aaye unhein rakhein; jin ne bohat views liye lekin messages nahi laye unhein band karein; phir nayi creatives test karein.

6. Message se order kaise lein

  • Option A: customer ko product ka link bhejein (Product-Links.txt), wo website par COD order de de.
  • Option B: Shopify Admin → Orders → Create order: customer ka naam, phone, address, city aur product daal kar order bana dein (Cash on Delivery).
  • Delivery free hai, dispatch confirmation ke 1–2 working days mein. Delivery 3–5 (bade shehar) aur 5–7 (doosre) working days ka andaza hai, guarantee nahi.
  • Advance Cash Payment (Rs. 499 off) sirf tab batayein jab Shopify mein uska payment method theek se bana aur test ho chuka ho. Payment verify hone se pehle order dispatch na karein, aur har ADVANCE499 wale order ka payment method dekh lein.

7. Phase 2: Shop Now / Sales ads (agla marhala)

Shart: Meta Events Manager mein Pixel ke events (ViewContent, AddToCart, InitiateCheckout, Purchase) verify ho chuke hon. Purchase event ke baghair "Shop now" sales ads ka koi faida nahi.
  • Objective: Sales → Conversion location: Website → Optimization: Purchases. Call to action: Shop now.
  • Meta ke mutabiq optimization ko seekhne ke liye hafte mein takreeban 50 events chahiye hote hain. Agar orders kam hon to shuru mein "Add to cart" ya "Initiate checkout" par optimize kar sakte hain.
  • Catalog ads (Advantage+ catalog): Facebook & Instagram channel se 15 products Meta catalog mein sync hain (aap ke screenshot ke mutabiq Approved).
  • Retargeting: website visitors aur wo log jinhon ne WhatsApp par message kiya, un ke liye alag ad set.

8. Ads reject hone se bachne ke liye

  • Sirf sach likhein. Jhoota discount, jhoota "sirf aaj" countdown, jhoote reviews/ratings, "100% original" jaise daawe na karein. (In creatives mein ye nahi hain.)
  • Kisi ko uski zaati sifat se mukhatib na karein (jaise "kya aap mote hain?" type). Hamare ads sirf product dikhate hain.
  • Landing aur WhatsApp par wohi qeemat batayein jo ad mein hai. Free delivery all Pakistan site par sach hai.
  • Ad reject ho to reason mujhe bhej dein, main wording theek kar dunga.
